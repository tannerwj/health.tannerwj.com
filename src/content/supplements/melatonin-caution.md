Matthew Walker's point in [this conversation](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/dr-matthew-walker-the-science-and-practice-of-perfecting-your-sleep) is that melatonin tells your body night is coming. It is a timing signal, not a sedative - which is why it helps with jet lag and disappoints people using it for ordinary insomnia.

The reason it is on this page rather than in my stack: most melatonin sold over the counter is dosed far above what that timing signal actually needs.
