The [sleep toolkit](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/toolkit-for-sleep) lists glycine at 2 g, three or four nights a week, in the group that comes *after* light, timing, and temperature - not as a foundation.

Worth remembering if you see glycine sold on claims about sleep stages: a nightly stage score from a tracker is not good enough to tell you whether it is working for you.
