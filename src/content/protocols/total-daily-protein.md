The RDA exists to prevent deficiency, which is a different question from what supports muscle — [Attia's point](https://x.com/PeterAttiaMD/status/1959616791270863084) exactly. The [ISSN position stand](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28698222/) lands considerably higher for people who train.

It matters most during fat loss, where [Norton](https://biolayne.com/podcasts/dr-layne-norton-podcast/what-really-matters-for-fat-loss-episode-6/) puts protein near the top: in a deficit, it is much of what decides whether the weight you lose is fat or muscle.
