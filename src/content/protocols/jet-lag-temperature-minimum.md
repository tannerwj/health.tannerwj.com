The useful concept in the [jet-lag guide](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/defeat-jet-lag) is the temperature minimum, roughly two hours before your usual wake time. Light in the hours after it advances your clock; light before it delays your clock.

Which means eastward and westward travel need opposite strategies, and doing the intuitive thing — bright light whenever you feel awake — can push you the wrong way.
