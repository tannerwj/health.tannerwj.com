The value of [DEXA](https://peterattiamd.com/ai-dexa-and-mortality/) over the bathroom scale is that it distinguishes fat from lean mass, flags visceral fat specifically, and reports bone density along the way.

It reports to a precision it does not have. Hydration and timing move the numbers, so scan under similar conditions and compare across scans rather than agonizing over a single result.
