[Norton's article](https://biolayne.com/articles/nutrition/fat-facts-fat-source-matters/) pushes back on treating dietary fat as one macro to hit. The unsaturated-to-saturated balance and the foods carrying it still show up in cardiovascular markers, even when calories match.

Which is a middle position between the low-fat era and the current enthusiasm for eating butter by the spoonful.
