[Walker's insomnia guide](https://themattwalkerpodcast.buzzsprout.com/1821163/episodes/18530776-121-a-practical-guide-to-insomnia) draws the line between a few bad nights and persistent insomnia, which is a distinct condition with an actual treatment.

CBT-I is that treatment, and it beats medication over the long run. It also is not what most people mean by sleep hygiene — it involves restricting time in bed, which is counterintuitive enough that it is worth doing with a program or clinician rather than improvising.
