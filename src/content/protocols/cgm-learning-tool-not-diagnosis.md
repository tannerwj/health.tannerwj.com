The [review of CGM use in people without diabetes](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10658694/) finds the evidence for wellness use is thin, while allowing that seeing your own response to particular meals can be genuinely instructive.

Glucose is supposed to rise after eating. Normal ranges in healthy people are wider than the apps imply, and reading a post-meal rise as damage is the standard way to end up anxious about food for no reason.
