The sleep mechanism is the rebound: warming up drives a steeper drop in core temperature afterward, and that drop is part of the signal to sleep. Same reason a [cool room](/sleep#cool-sleep-environment) helps.

The bigger sauna claims lean on the [Finnish cohort](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25705824/), which is observational — people who sit in saunas several times a week differ in other ways too. Patrick's own [correction on temperature targets](https://x.com/foundmyfitness/status/2054616638863904788) is worth reading, since the popular numbers are far more precise than the evidence.
