When calories are matched, [the timing effect largely disappears](https://biolayne.com/reps/issue-5/can-timing-your-meals-enhance-fat-loss/). What a shorter window does is make it harder to overeat, which is a real benefit but not the mechanism it is usually sold as.

[Attia's objection](https://x.com/PeterAttiaMD/status/2000929211272323563) is worth weighing if you lift: a narrow window makes adequate protein harder, and he flags a [possible lean-mass cost](https://x.com/PeterAttiaMD/status/1936061768167477571). If the schedule suits you, fine. If you are forcing it, you are paying for nothing.
