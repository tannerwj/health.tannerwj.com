[Norton](https://biolayne.com/podcasts/dr-layne-norton-podcast/things-ive-changed-my-mind-on-episode-5/) lists this among the things he changed his mind about. Frequent small meals do not stoke your metabolism, and fewer larger meals are not inherently cleaner.

Choose by appetite, schedule, and training. It is a preference, not a lever.
