Lp(a) is set mostly by genetics and does not move much with diet or exercise, which is why the [EAS consensus](https://eas-society.org/page/lipoproteina-consensus-2022/) recommends measuring it once in a lifetime. A high value does not change day to day, but it changes how aggressively everything else should be managed.

Drugs targeting it specifically are still in trials. For now the value is context — particularly if heart disease shows up early in your family and nobody has explained why.
