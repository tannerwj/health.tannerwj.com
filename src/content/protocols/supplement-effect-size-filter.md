[Norton's filter](https://x.com/BioLayne/status/2054964281842569358) is two questions, and a supplement has to pass both. Plenty of things have a statistically real effect that is far too small to notice. His [tier list](https://biolayne.com/podcasts/dr-layne-norton-podcast/supplement-series-tier-1-episode-22/) is short at the top for that reason — creatine and protein clear it, most of the shelf does not.

[Huberman lands in the same place](https://x.com/hubermanlab/status/2063326890421813260) from the other direction: behaviors before compounds. Sleep and training beat everything in the cupboard, which is worth remembering given how much of this site links to Amazon.
