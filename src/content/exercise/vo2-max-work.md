VO2 max is one of the strongest predictors in the longevity literature, which is why [Attia](https://peterattiamd.com/exercising-for-longevity-peter-on-zone-2-and-zone-5-training/) keeps hard intervals in the plan. The catch is that the dose is small — this is the seasoning, not the meal.

Galpin's [program-design work](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/dr-andy-galpin-optimize-your-training-program-for-fitness-and-longevity) is the useful counterweight to copying someone else's interval split. Distribute intensity against your own recovery, not against a plan you read about.
