The mistake with power work is treating it like everything else in the gym, where fatigue is the point. Galpin frames it the opposite way: you are training the nervous system to produce force fast, and a slow rep trains the wrong thing.

Which makes rest the actual variable. Rest longer than feels reasonable, and stop the moment the bar or your body visibly slows — the remaining sets are not just wasted, they are teaching you to be slow.
