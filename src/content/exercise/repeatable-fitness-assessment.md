Galpin's [assessment episode](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/dr-andy-galpin-how-to-assess-improve-all-aspects-of-your-fitness) lays out a battery covering strength, endurance, power, speed, and mobility. The point is not the scores — it is that most people have one obviously weak quality and have been avoiding it for years.

Whatever you test, test it the same way next time. A number you cannot reproduce is not a measurement.
