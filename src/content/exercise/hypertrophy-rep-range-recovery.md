Galpin's [point about mechanical tension](https://x.com/DrAndyGalpin/status/2024085990273433923) is the one that matters: the muscle responds to tension and effort, not to a rep count. Five reps and twenty reps both work if the set is genuinely hard, which is why [lighter loads still build muscle](https://x.com/foundmyfitness/status/2011104607015940392).

That frees you to pick the rep range that suits the exercise. Heavy triples on a leg press are miserable and risky; twenty-rep sets on a heavy squat are a different kind of miserable. Match the range to the movement, then make sure you can recover from the total.
