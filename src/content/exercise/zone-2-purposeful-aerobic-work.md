Attia is [explicit about this](https://x.com/PeterAttiaMD/status/1961066394830647436): Zone 2 is not a number you get from subtracting your age from 220. It is the pace you could hold for an hour while still holding a conversation, and the 220-minus-age formula is wrong often enough to be useless for an individual.

The [Zone 2 and Zone 5 piece](https://peterattiamd.com/exercising-for-longevity-peter-on-zone-2-and-zone-5-training/) covers how it pairs with hard work. Galpin's addition is that when cardio and lifting share a week, session order and spacing decide whether both get done well.
