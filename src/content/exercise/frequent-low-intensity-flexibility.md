Huberman's [approach](https://x.com/hubermanlab/status/2067748104766386596) is a few minutes at a time, well below maximal, often. The appeal is that it fits anywhere and does not need to be a session you schedule and then skip.

His own practice, offered as such rather than as a protocol.
