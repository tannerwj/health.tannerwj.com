Galpin's [take](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/dr-andy-galpin-optimal-protocols-to-build-strength-and-grow-muscles) is that a warm-up should rehearse the thing you are about to do. Ramp-up sets of the actual movement do more than a general routine that has nothing to do with the session.

Scale it to the demand. An easy session needs a few minutes; a heavy or fast one earns real preparation. The failure mode is a warm-up long enough to count as its own workout.
