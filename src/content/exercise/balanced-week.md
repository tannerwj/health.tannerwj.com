Attia's [framework](https://peterattiamd.com/exercising-for-longevity/) is the antidote to picking one thing and doing only that. Strength, sustainable aerobic work, higher-intensity work, and movement capacity are different adaptations, and being excellent at one does not cover the others.

The balance shifts with your goal and the time you have. The version that survives contact with a real schedule beats the optimal one you abandon in March.
