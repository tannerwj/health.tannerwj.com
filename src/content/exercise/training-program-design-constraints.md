Galpin's [program-design episode](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/dr-andy-galpin-optimize-your-training-program-for-fitness-and-longevity) works backward from reality: name the goal, then fit exercise selection, frequency, volume, intensity, and deloads into the time and recovery you actually have.

The part most plans skip is slack for bad sleep, travel, and missed sessions. A block that assumes nothing goes wrong gets abandoned the first week something does.
