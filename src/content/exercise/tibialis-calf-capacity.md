Patrick [splits the lower leg](https://kneesovertoesguy.substack.com/p/atg-knee-ability-series-4) into tibialis, straight-leg calf, and bent-leg calf. The split matters because the straight-leg and bent-leg versions hit different muscles, and doing only one leaves a gap most people have.

The tibialis is the one nobody trains and the one that tends to be embarrassingly weak. Wall-supported raises need no equipment and are a fine place to find out.
