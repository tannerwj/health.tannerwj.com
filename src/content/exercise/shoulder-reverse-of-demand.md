Patrick's [reverse-of-demand heuristic](https://www.atgonlinecoaching.com/articles/article-4-shoulder-health-tests-and-skills-for-life) is a simple way to pick accessory work: whatever you do a lot of, train the opposite. Heavy pressing gets balanced with external rotation and rowing. [Galpin says the same thing](https://x.com/DrAndyGalpin/status/2072049295684198565) in different words.

Start lighter than you think for external rotation — it is a small muscle group and everyone overloads it first time. The ATG "tests" in that article are coaching benchmarks, not diagnostics; a painful shoulder needs an actual assessment.
