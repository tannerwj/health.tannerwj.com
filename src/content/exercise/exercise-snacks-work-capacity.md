Patrick's [use case](https://x.com/foundmyfitness/status/2035004550440993258) is the strongest one: a few minutes of movement to interrupt a long sedentary block, where the comparison is not to a workout but to continuing to sit.

Keep them easy enough that they do not tax the sessions that matter. Huberman's [fitness-tools rundown](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/science-supported-tools-to-accelerate-your-fitness-goals) treats these as additions to a program, and that is the honest framing — a stair sprint is not a training block.
