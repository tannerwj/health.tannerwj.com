[Attia's framing](https://peterattiamd.com/training-for-the-centenarian-decathlon/) starts with tasks rather than exercises. Lift a grandchild. Get off the floor unassisted. Carry luggage up stairs. Hike four miles. Each maps back to a trainable capacity, and now the gym has a point.

It is worth actually writing the list. Your ten tasks will not be someone else's, and it makes training decisions obvious in a way that generic longevity advice never does.
