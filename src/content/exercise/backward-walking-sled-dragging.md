Backward walking is the ATG on-ramp, and its appeal is mechanical: dragging a sled backward has almost no eccentric component, so it loads the knee without the braking forces that usually aggravate it. Patrick's [knee ability series](https://kneesovertoesguy.substack.com/p/atg-knee-ability-series-2-of-10) puts it first for that reason.

No sled needed to start — backward walking on a treadmill or up a hill works. Progress one variable at a time: distance, then incline, then load.
