The gap is the whole story. BPC-157 is everywhere in injury-recovery discussion, and [Huberman is direct](https://x.com/hubermanlab/status/2063326058884264124) that the randomized human trials do not exist. The animal work is genuinely interesting, which is not the same as knowing what it does in people.
