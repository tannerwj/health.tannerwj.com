Walker [names the distinction](https://x.com/sleepdiplomat/status/2059718783850430784): insomnia is having the opportunity and not being able to sleep. Bedtime procrastination is never giving yourself the opportunity, usually because the evening is the only unclaimed time in the day.

Worth being honest about which one you have, because the second is a scheduling problem and the first is not.
