Huberman keeps coming back to this one, both in the [sleep toolkit](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/toolkit-for-sleep) and [directly](https://x.com/hubermanlab/status/1737627073626407105): outdoor light early in the day is the signal that sets everything downstream. Satchin Panda's [conversation with FoundMyFitness](https://www.foundmyfitness.com/episodes/satchin-panda-3) puts it alongside meal timing as the pair that anchors your daily rhythm.

Outdoors beats a window, and a cloudy morning outside still beats a bright room. When that is not possible, the [10,000-lux lamp](#10000-lux-light) is the fallback.
