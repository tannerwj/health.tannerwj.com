Ten hours sounds extreme next to the usual "nothing after 2pm," but caffeine has a long tail and the cost shows up as worse sleep quality rather than trouble falling asleep — which is exactly why people fail to connect the two. [Huberman](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/improve-your-sleep) and [Walker](https://www.foundmyfitness.com/episodes/matthew-walker) both flag afternoon caffeine for that reason.

People clear it at very different rates, so the right number is personal. Mine is about ten hours; if you are unsure, move yours earlier and see what changes.
