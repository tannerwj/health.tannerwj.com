Walker's [protocols conversation](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/guest-series-dr-matthew-walker-protocols-to-improve-your-sleep) covers the parts of a wind-down people skip: the visible clock that turns a bad night into a math problem, the phone within reach, and having something to do with racing thoughts other than lying there. Pair it with keeping [evening light dim](#darkness-and-dim-evening-light).

The other half is bedtime procrastination, which Walker [separates from insomnia](https://x.com/sleepdiplomat/status/2059718783850430784): insomnia is having the opportunity and being unable to sleep; procrastination is never giving yourself the opportunity, usually because the evening is the only unclaimed time in the day. Worth knowing which one you have, because the second is a scheduling problem and the first is not.
