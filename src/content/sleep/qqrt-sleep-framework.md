Walker's framing in [this conversation](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/guest-series-dr-matthew-walker-the-biology-of-sleep-your-unique-sleep-needs) is that sleep has four dimensions and most people track one. Eight hours at random times is not the same as seven hours on a schedule.

It is also the cleanest argument against copying someone else's bedtime, since chronotype and age move the timing dimension around.
