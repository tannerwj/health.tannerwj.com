Patrick's [three-hour rule](https://x.com/foundmyfitness/status/2025967196644495503) is her own preference rather than a study result, and it is the easier of the two to test: move your largest meal earlier and see whether bedtime feels different. Reflux, shift work, and evening training all shift that number.

Separately, she [highlights research](https://x.com/foundmyfitness/status/2029273849397952567) linking higher fiber and more plant variety to better sleep measures. That one is an association, and diet quality travels with a lot of other things people who sleep well tend to do — so treat it as a nutrition reason rather than something you can act on tonight.
