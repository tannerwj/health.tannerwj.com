The distinction [Huberman](https://www.hubermanlab.com/newsletter/improve-your-sleep) and [FoundMyFitness](https://www.foundmyfitness.com/episodes/alcohol-and-other-factors-that-negatively-affect-sleep) both draw: alcohol makes falling asleep feel easier while fragmenting the second half of the night and suppressing REM.

This is the one that most reliably shows up on a tracker the next morning, if you needed convincing.
